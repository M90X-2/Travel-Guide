Tips for Easy, Stress-Free Traveling

Airport Security Prep: follow the 3-1-1 liquids rule, keep electronics handy, and wear easy to take off shoes to speed up screening.

Packing Basics

  • Roll clothes and pack layers
  • Keep Travel-size toiletries in a clear pouch
  • Reusable bottles
  • small first-aid kit

Budget & Planning

  • Set a daily spend limit for meals/transport
  • Book tickets in advance
  • Save maps and key addresses
  • Keep a small emergency fund

Safety

  • Share your itinerary with a friend
  • Keep copies of your ID/insurance
  • Use hotel safes
  • Memorize local emergency numbers

Sustainable Travel

  • Skip single-use plastics
  • Walk or use public transit
  • Respect wildlife and trails
